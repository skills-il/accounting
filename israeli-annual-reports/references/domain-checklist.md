# Domain Checklist: Reading & Analyzing Israeli Public-Company Annual Reports

Scope: guiding an analyst/CPA to find, read, and interpret a TASE-listed
company's periodic (תקופתי) filing on MAYA/MAGNA. Each item cited.

## Must cover (a skill that omits any of these can materially mislead an analyst)

1. **Where to read vs. where issuers file.** MAYA (`maya.tase.co.il`) is the
   public viewer; MAGNA (`www.magna.isa.gov.il`) is the issuer-side ISA
   submission system. Source: ISA / TASE, https://maya.tase.co.il ,
   https://www.magna.isa.gov.il
2. **The five core financial statements + ביאורים.** Balance sheet (מאזן /
   דוח על המצב הכספי), P&L (רווח והפסד / רווח כולל), cash flow (תזרים),
   statement of changes in equity (דוח על השינויים בהון), AND the notes
   (ביאורים) where the analytic detail lives. Source: IAS 1 presentation set,
   applied via IFRS adoption, https://www.ifrs.org/use-around-the-world/use-of-ifrs-standards-by-jurisdiction/view-jurisdiction/israel/
3. **Report types + deadlines.** Annual/periodic (3 months), quarterly
   (interim, two months from the report date and within 3 days of the auditor's review-report signature, Reg. 39(a)), immediate (miyadi, Reg. 30(b): 09:30 next trading day for an event learned 09:30-17:00; 13:00 next trading day if learned after 17:00 or on a non-trading day; 13:00 the SAME day if learned up to 09:30 on a trading day). Source: Securities Regs (Periodic
   and Immediate Reports) 5730-1970, Reg. 7 / Reg. 39, https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/308_014.htm
4. **Half-yearly substitute for small corporations.** A תאגיד קטן whose debt
   is not publicly held is exempt from Q1/Q3 and files a half-yearly report
   instead. Source: 2017 amendment + ISA legal position 104-18, https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_corporations_asset_library/corporations_pdf_pages-62eb9004f19c3/240417.pdf ;
   https://www.bdo.co.il/he-il/כתבות-ומאמרים/ביקורת-וראיית-חשבון/מחלקה-מקצועית/עמדה-משפטית-מספר-104-18-מתכונת-הדיווח-של-תאגיד-קט (עמדה משפטית 104-18)
5. **IFRS adoption (2008) and the pre-2008 Israeli-GAAP boundary.** Source:
   IFRS Foundation Israel jurisdiction profile, https://www.ifrs.org/use-around-the-world/use-of-ifrs-standards-by-jurisdiction/view-jurisdiction/israel/
6. **Bank exception.** Banks report under the Bank of Israel Supervisor of
   Banks Public Reporting Directives, an Israel-specific template that draws
   on mainly the reporting rules of leading US banks (FASB, US bank
   supervisors, SEC), not plain IFRS. Source: BOI Supervisor of Banks Public
   Reporting Directives, https://www.boi.org.il
7. **Dual-listed / foreign-corporation exception.** A תאגיד חוץ traded abroad
   may report under foreign disclosure rules (הדין הזר). Governed by Chapter
   ה'3 of the Securities Law 1968 (ss. 35יז-35לג), a framework, not a single
   numbered regulation. (Distinct from Chapter ה'3א, which is TASE *listing*
   of foreign-traded shares, not the disclosure regime.) Source: Securities
   Law 5728-1968, Ch. ה'3, https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/308_001.htm
8. **Auditor's report, read it before trusting the numbers, and know all
   opinion types.** Four opinion types per Israeli auditing standards
   (תקני ביקורת 700/705): unqualified (בלתי מסויגת/נקייה), qualified
   (בהסתייגות), adverse (חוות דעת שלילית), disclaimer (הימנעות מחוות דעת).
   Going-concern (הערת עסק חי) is an emphasis-of-matter paragraph
   (a paragraph added at the end of the report), NOT an opinion grade WHERE DISCLOSURE IS ADEQUATE; where the going-concern disclosure is inadequate ICPAS 570 requires a qualified or adverse opinion. Source: ISA(IL) 700/705/570,
   ICPAS, https://icpas.org.il/professional_updates/תקן-ביקורת-ישראל-700-3/ (תקן ביקורת ישראל 700, 705)
9. **Correct core Hebrew terminology** (maazan, revach naki vs revach golmi,
   ביאורים, etc.). Source: standard IFRS-IL terminology / ICPAS glossary.

## Should cover (incomplete without these, but not actively misleading)

10. **Small-corp relief is a bundle, not just half-yearly.** A תאגיד קטן
    (avg. share market value < NIS 335,519,486 on the determining date, the
    regulation's NIS 300M CPI-indexed by ISA notice from 1 January 2024; for an
    issuer with public bonds, bonds in circulation < NIS 200M par; and not
    included in one of the TASE leading share indices derived from market cap;
    Reg. 5ג) also gets: exemption from the internal-control
    (ISOX) report, raised valuation-attachment materiality threshold (20% vs
    10%), associated-company statements threshold 40% instead of 20%, and
    exemption from Reg. 9ג solo data for an issuer without public bonds (Reg.
    5ד(ב)(6)); note Reg. 9ג also never applies to banks, credit-card companies
    or insurers (9ג(ב)) and may be omitted where immaterial (9ג(ג)). The market-risk relief (old head (3)) was deleted in 2017. Since
    2017 the reliefs apply by default and the issuer discloses any provisions
    it chose NOT to apply on the first page (Reg. 5ה(ב)). Reg. 5ג(ב) band:
    NIS 279,599,572 to enter, NIS 391,439,401 to stay (indexed amounts). Source: Securities Regs 5730-1970 Reg. 5ג +
    Reg. 5ד, https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/308_014.htm ; Grant Thornton
    client alert, https://www.grantthornton.co.il/insights1/professional-insights/professional-insights-2014/---62014/
11. **ISA 701 Key Audit Matters (ענייני מפתח בביקורת)** in the auditor's
    report of larger issuers, where the auditor flags the riskiest estimates.
    Source: תקן ביקורת ישראל 701, https://icpas.org.il/professional_updates/תקן-ביקורת-ישראל-701/
12. **Periodic-report timing nuance:** the periodic report must also be filed
    at least 14 days before the AGM that receives the statements, or within 3
    days of the auditor's opinion signature date, whichever is earlier (not
    just "within 3 months"); Reg. 7(b) separately caps the gap between the
    report's own signature date and filing at 3 days. Source: Securities Regs 5730-1970 Reg. 7, https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/308_014.htm
13. **Search by securities number (mispar niyar), not name**, on MAYA. Source:
    operational MAYA behavior, https://maya.tase.co.il
14. **Board report (דוח הדירקטוריון) Reg. 10 content**, MD&A, liquidity, and
    the period's material events; and Parts D/E (Part E = the Reg. 8(b) fifth chapter, the ISOX report with the CEO/CFO certifications attached under Reg. 9ב(ד); Part D = additional information /
    officer-comp Reg. 21). Source: Securities Regs 5730-1970, Regs. 8b-10,
    21, https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/308_014.htm
15. **iXBRL / structured filing**: VOLUNTARY as of the ISA 2026 regulatory
    plan (29.12.2025), which lists the enabling amendment (scope and timetable
    of mandatory iXBRL) as planned, not enacted. Sources:
    https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_lobby/al_lobby-628ce07f9490d/ASDA2026.pdf ,
    https://www.new.isa.gov.il/images/Fittings/isa/asset_library_pic/al_lobby/al_lobby-63c5442fc580d/IXBRL042023.pdf
16. **Shelf prospectus term**: offers within 24 months, the ISA extends by 12 where the Sec. 23A(d) conditions are met;
    commercial-paper-only shelf 12 months; an offering is generally made by a
    shelf offering report unless all its terms were fixed in the prospectus. Source: Securities Law Sec. 23A(b), (c), (f),
    https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/308_001.htm
17. **Pending disclosure reform**: final committee report 16 June 2026,
    revised draft regulations 6 October 2026, not in force. Describe filings
    under the current regulations. Sources: https://www.shibolet.com/?p=26254 ,
    https://www.funder.co.il/article/211159

## Out of scope (do not penalize for omitting)

- Tax-return (מס הכנסה) filing mechanics (Forms 1301 / 1214 / 6111). Re-litigated
  2026-10-18: users DO ask, because "דוח שנתי" also means the tax return, so
  the skill now names the forms and routes to `israeli-tax-returns`, which
  carries the deadlines. Deadlines are deliberately not duplicated here.
- The Registrar of Companies annual report (Companies Law Sec. 141). Re-litigated
  2026-10-18: users ask, so the skill routes them to the Corporations
  Authority; the filing itself stays out of scope.
- Companies Law governance mechanics beyond what a reader meets in Part D
  (external/independent directors Secs. 219/239/240, audit-committee duties
  Sec. 117, chair-CEO separation Sec. 121, auditor appointment Secs. 154-167,
  merger procedure Secs. 320-327), and Magna filer-side mechanics (filer
  registration, electronic-signature certificates, form codes). Reviewed
  2026-10-18: this skill serves people READING filings; issuers filing on Magna
  work through counsel and the ISA's own filer guidance, and the governance
  sections are a corporate-law topic, not report reading.
- Private-company (non-reporting) Companies-Law Sec. 172 statements beyond a
  one-line contrast.
- Step-by-step IFRS measurement tutorials (the skill points to ifrs.org).
- US SEC / 20-F mechanics except as the foreign regime a dual-listed issuer
  may rely on.
