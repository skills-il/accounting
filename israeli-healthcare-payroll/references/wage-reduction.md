# The temporary wage reduction in the health dirugim

The war-cost participation deduction, agreed by collective agreement on
25.11.2024 and separately legislated in 2025. Three tracks apply across the
health dirugim. SKILL.md carries the summary; this file carries the detail.

## The three tracks

A war-cost participation
  deduction, agreed by collective agreement on 25.11.2024 and separately
  legislated in 2025. There are THREE tracks with different rates, and which
  one applies depends on whether the worker is covered by the collective
  agreement. Histadrut-represented healthcare dirugim are on the agreement track;
  workers outside an approved collective agreement fall under the law track. The
  doctors' dirug is on a THIRD track of its own under the agreement of 23.1.2025
  (a freeze of the seniority supplement and of index linkage), not the 31.3.2025
  wage agreement, and its rates are lower than either general track: 1.081% from
  1.1.2025, raised to 1.781% for 1.7.2025 to 31.12.2025 by the monitoring
  committee's decision of 30.6.2025, then back to 1.081% for all of 2026. Doctors'
  havraa was also cut separately under the agreement of 14.5.2025: by 66.2% in
  2025 and by 5% in 2026.

| Period | By agreement | By law |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| December 2024 to March 2025 | 2.290% | 0.000% |
| April 2025 to December 2025 | 2.290% | 3.307% |
| January 2026 to December 2026 | 1.200% | 1.200% |

  The two tracks converge at 1.200% for 2026 and the reduction ends 31.12.2026.
  A worker who changes dirug between 1.4.2025 and 31.12.2025 KEEPS the track they
  started on, even when the other track's rate is higher.

  It is computed on the salary excluding havraa, the clothing allowance, expense
  reimbursements, and payments not made monthly, weekly or daily; mena'ak yovel
  IS in the base. It shows on the slip as "tikun pensioni" and also reduces the
  keren hishtalmut base. It does NOT reduce a one-off end-of-employment payment to
  the worker or their survivors (severance included), bridging payments or an
  employer-funded annuity top-up, the amount paid into a pension fund, the
  mashkoret koveat for budgetary pension, or budgetary pension payments: the
  agreement lists those expressly and requires them to be computed as if the
  reduction had never been made. Section 8 of the law carries the same list and
  adds the income used for the tax treatment of pension contributions (sections
  3(e3), 9(7a), 45a and 47 of the Income Tax Ordinance, except for payments for
  tax year 2024), disability-insurance premiums, and National Insurance benefits
  under signs E and H of chapter E of the National Insurance Law.

  Income tax: the law reduces the salary itself (section 2), and defines salary
  as employment income under section 2(2) of the Income Tax Ordinance paid in
  money. Section 8(3) neutralises the reduction only for the tax treatment of
  pension contributions. On the statute's structure, then, the reduced salary is
  what is taxed, except in those pension-contribution computations, which run as
  if no reduction had been made. No Tax Authority instruction says so expressly:
  the employer reports the amount in Tofes 126, and the Accountant General's page
  says the Tax Authority will publish separate Form 106 instructions. Reconcile
  against the slip's income-tax line.

  Mena'ak yovel is in the base: in 2026 a 1.2% reduction (the agreement and law
  rate) is taken from it on a separate slip line titled "tikun pensioni begin
  rechiv shnati". The circular gives no doctors'-track rate for yovel; for a
  doctor, read the line from the slip.

  The law's second period ends 31.12.2026. As of 19.10.2026 no extension into 2027
  had been agreed or enacted.
  A 2025 or 2026 slip read without this line overstates net pay.
