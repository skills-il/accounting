# Pension and severance base, annual lines, and "why is this month lower?"

SKILL.md Step 4 carries the summary. This file carries the detail and the sources.

## Pension and severance base: split by pension type and employer

There is no single "pensionable" list for a healthcare slip. Pin the pension type
and the employer first.

| Worker | Deposit or determining base | Source |
|--------|-----------------------------|--------|
| Budgetary pension (pensia taktzivit), veteran state employees | The mashkoret koveat, a narrow defined subset of the monthly salary that does NOT include havraa. Read it from the employer; do not assume any supplement is inside it. | AG instruction 13.7.2, para 3.3.1 (it ADDS havraa and yovel to the budgetary determining salary, so they are not in it) |
| State employees on a funded pension (government-hospital staff covered by the transfer agreement of 3.3.1999) | Tier 1: the budgetary-style determining salary excluding clothing and INCLUDING mena'ak yovel and havraa, plus every other monthly salary component that is not an expense reimbursement, tax gross-up or benefit imputation, except clothing. Tier 2: pay for additional work. Both tiers carry employee, employer and severance deposits (6% in the pension-fund tables; the managers-insurance tables carry different rates). | Accountant General instruction 13.7.2 "הפקדות לקופת גמל", edition 03, in force from 22.12.2011, paras 1.1, 3.3 to 3.4 and the deposit tables |
| Severance the state pays directly | Includes one twelfth of the havraa at the quota due on the last day of service, CPI-linked to the month severance is paid. | Takshir 28.245, 28.246 |
| Clalit, Hadassah, municipal hospitals | Read the employer's own pension arrangement. None of the above is shown to bind them. | (no source carried) |

Consequence: on a state funded pension the tier definitions are broad (havraa and
most monthly lines are inside them), but whether a given line carries deposits
depends on the applicable collective agreement (para 4.1 of the instruction):
the 80 NIS shift-responsibility supplement is expressly excluded, and the IMA
agreement says doctors' planned duty and on-call are not part of the salary. The older rule of thumb
"shifts, on-call and havraa are not pensionable" does not hold as a blanket rule:
check the agreement for each line. For a
budgetary pension only the exclusion of havraa is sourced here.

The instruction is dated 22.12.2011. Confirm it is still the current edition with
the employer's payroll department before quoting a deposit figure from it.

Named lines with their own rule regardless of pension type:

- Tosefet achrayut mishmarot (80 NIS per shift): expressly NOT salary for the
  hourly rate, overtime, severance or pension.
- Allied-health retention grant and the nurses' psychiatric and geriatric grants:
  expressly NOT salary for any purpose.
- Tosefet mesima leumit (doctors): salary for severance, carries keren
  hishtalmut, NOT in the mashkoret koveat.
- Mena'ak yovel: on a budgetary pension it is not determining salary and carries
  no provident-fund or keren hishtalmut deposits; on a funded pension only, the
  2026 circular puts it in the second-tier (additional work) deposits. Instruction
  13.7.2 para 3.3.1 lists it in the tier-1 base instead. The two sources disagree;
  read the slip.

## Havraa

- Rate: the PUBLIC-sector day rate, 511.60 NIS from 1.6.2026 (471.40 before),
  per the Commissioner on Wages circular of 8.6.2026. Never the private-sector
  451.50.
- Timing: usually paid in one of the summer months (June to September) unless the
  workplace has another date. By October the year's havraa has normally been paid.
- Days: Takshir 28.241 for state employees, in `havraa-days.md`.
- Partial year: years are counted from 1 January; an employee who started after
  1 January gets havraa pro-rated by days actually worked in the year / 365
  (Takshir 28.243).
- Doctors: the public rate, then cut under the agreement of 14.5.2025: by 66.2%
  in 2025 and by 5% in 2026.

## Clothing allowance (ktzuvat bigud)

2026: 1,812.00 NIS at level 3 and 2,527.00 NIS at level 4, updated 1.9455% over
2025 by the circular of 17.6.2026, paid in the July 2026 salary, pro-rated by
period worked and position. Under Takshir 28.425, level 3 is up to administrative
grade 16 or unified grade 7 (and parallel grades in the professional dirugim);
level 4 is administrative grade 17 or unified grade 8 and above. Not pensionable
in either tier.

## Mena'ak yovel (long-service award)

- Paid every year once the seniority test is met: 25 years, or 30 in the doctors'
  dirug. Accelerated seniority (a second or third degree, certificate studies)
  does not count.
- Amount: 60% of the salary forming the hourly-rate base, pro-rated by position
  and working days in the entitlement year. Tosefet achayot 2024 counts toward it.
- Month: most public dirugim in the September salary; nurses in the November
  salary.
- Wage reduction: in the base. In 2026 a 1.2% reduction is taken from it on a
  separate line, "tikun pensioni begin rechiv shnati". That is the agreement and
  law rate; the circular gives no doctors'-track rate for yovel, so for a doctor
  read the line from the slip.

## Nurses' setting grants (agreement of 11.12.2023)

- Named psychiatric hospitals: 5,000 NIS per full-time nurse, pro-rated for part
  time, paid once in the February 2024 salary.
- Named geriatric hospitals: 5,000 NIS per full-time nurse after 12 continuous
  months in the preceding calendar year, paid every year in the January salary.
- New nurses and nurses retiring for age in those hospitals: 5,000 NIS divided by
  the months worked in the qualifying period (the retiring nurse's is paid in the
  final settlement).
- None of these grants is salary for any purpose (no hourly rate, overtime,
  percentage supplements or severance).

## Retroactive pay (hefreshei sachar)

Almost every agreement here was signed after its own effective date, so money
arrives as a back-payment lump. The temporary wage reduction does NOT apply to
arrears paid for periods before its first period.

## "Why is this month lower?" checklist

Compare the month against the previous ones line by line before concluding
anything is wrong:

1. Annual lines that paid in an earlier month: havraa (summer), clothing (July),
   mena'ak yovel (September, or November for nurses), the geriatric grant
   (January).
2. A retro lump in the earlier month, for example for the April 2026 framework
   tranche.
3. Fewer shifts, on-call or duty slots, which are paid as worked.
4. Position fraction or unpaid absence in the month.
5. The temporary wage reduction: in 2026 it is 1.2% on the agreement and law
   tracks, LOWER than 2025's 2.290% or 3.307%, so it cannot explain a 2026 drop by
   itself.
