# Havraa days for state employees in the health dirugim

Source: Takshir (the civil-service regulations), para 28.241 to 28.247, read
2026-10-19 at https://www.gov.il/apps/nasham/takshir/takshir-tree?openItemId=5215
(a JavaScript app; render it in a browser). The day RATE is separate: 511.60 NIS
a day in the public sector from 1.6.2026 (see SKILL.md Step 4).

**Scope.** The Takshir binds STATE employees, which includes staff of government
hospitals. Clalit, Hadassah and municipal hospitals are not shown to follow these
tables. For those employers, read the days from the employer and do not assume
either these tables or the private-sector table.

## (b) Government hospitals: most health dirugim

Applies to staff employed in government hospitals in the administrative,
academic (mach'ar), engineers, practical-engineers and technicians, journalists,
psychologists, physiotherapists, lawyers, biochemists and microbiologists,
pharmacists, medical and nursing students, nurses, teaching, social work, speech
clinicians, para-medical, and occupational-therapy dirugim. Days are by
recognized seniority (vetek mukar). In force from 20.7.1989.

| Recognized seniority | Days |
|----------------------|------|
| First year | 9 |
| Years 2 to 4 | 11 |
| Years 5 to 10 | 12 |
| Years 11 to 15 | 13 |
| Years 16 to 20 | 14 |
| From year 21 | 15 |

Worked figure, 2026: a full-time government-hospital nurse with 12 years of
recognized seniority is in the years 11 to 15 row: 13 days x 511.60 NIS a day
(arithmetic on the two sourced figures: 6,650.80 in all, gross), normally paid in
the summer, so already paid by October.
Pro-rate for part time and for a partial year.

## (c) Allied health NOT in a government hospital

Occupational therapists, para-medical staff, physiotherapists and radiographers
employed by the state outside a government hospital.

| Years of service | Days |
|------------------|------|
| First 3 years | 6 |
| Years 4 to 10 | 8 |
| Years 11 to 15 | 9 |
| Years 16 to 19 | 10 |
| From year 20 | 11 |

## (d) Doctors' dirug

By years of service. **New table in force from 1.1.2026**; secondary sites still
show an older doctors' table, so do not copy one from them.

| Years of service | Days |
|------------------|------|
| First 3 years | 6 |
| Years 4 to 6 | 8 |
| Years 7 to 10 | 10 |
| Years 11 to 15 | 11 |
| Years 16 to 19 | 12 |
| From year 20 | 13 |

State-employed doctors' havraa is then reduced under the agreement of 14.5.2025:
by 66.2% in 2025 and by 5% in 2026.

## Negev (28.242)

An employee whose permanent workplace and base are in the Negev (as defined in
Takshir 25.411), with continuous years of work there, gets this table instead of
the ones above if it is better for them:

| Continuous years in the Negev | Days |
|-------------------------------|------|
| Up to 3 years | 9 |
| Years 4 to 7 | 10 |
| Years 8 to 11 | 11 |
| Years 12 to 24 | 12 |
| From year 25 | 13 |

## Rules that change the number

- Radiographers get 5 days more than the (b) or (c) table gives them (28.241(g)).
- An employee under 18 gets 8 days a year from the first year (28.241(f)).
- Part-timers are pro-rated by position fraction (28.247).
- Years are counted from 1 January; an employee who started after 1 January gets
  a partial havraa computed on an annual basis by days actually worked / 365
  (28.243).
- A year with more than six months of unpaid leave does not count as a year of
  work (28.244).
- **Severance.** One twelfth of the havraa, at the quota due on the day service
  ends, is included in the salary on which severance is computed (28.245, 28.246).
  For that purpose the twelfth is linked to the CPI of the month severance is
  paid, against the havraa as paid and the CPI of the month it was paid (28.245).
  This governs severance the state pays directly; on a funded pension, severance
  also accrues through the pension deposits (see `pension-and-annual-lines.md`).
  So havraa is not wholly outside a state employee's severance base.
