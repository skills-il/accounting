# Domain Checklist: Israeli Receipt / Tax-Invoice Scanning for Input-VAT (מס תשומות)

Scope of this skill: digitize Israeli receipts and tax invoices so a bookkeeper can (a) record the expense and (b) decide whether the input VAT is deductible and correctly claimable. The bar for "correct" is the input-VAT claim, not just data capture.

## Must cover (a wrong/missing item here can produce an over-claimed or rejected input-VAT deduction)

1. **Document-type classification drives deductibility**, only a חשבונית מס (or חשבונית מס/קבלה) supports input VAT; a קבלה, a חשבונית עסקה (proforma), and any osek-patur document do not. Source: Kol Zchut, הוצאת חשבונית מס, חשבונית עסקה וקבלה, https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/הוצאת_חשבונית_מס,_חשבונית_עסקה_וקבלה
2. **Supplier osek number captured and 9-digit-validated**, input VAT is deductible only against a tax invoice issued by a registered osek murshe; the supplier's 9-digit מספר עוסק must be on the invoice. Source: Kol Zchut, מס תשומות, https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/מס_תשומות
3. **osek-patur supplier flagged as carrying NO VAT**, an osek-patur issues no tax invoice and charges no VAT, so there is nothing to deduct; any VAT "extracted" from such a document is an error. Source: Kol Zchut, עוסק פטור, https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/עוסק_פטור
4. **Allocation number (מספר הקצאה) captured and threshold-checked**, input VAT is disallowed without an allocation number on tax invoices above the threshold (NIS 10,000 net Jan 2026; NIS 5,000 net from 1 Jun 2026; NIS 20,000 in 2025). Source: ACI, חובת מספר הקצאה בניכוי מס תשומות מ-2026, https://aci.org.il/knowledge/allocation-number-input-tax-2026/ ; Lishkat HaMischar T"A, https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/finance/1429/162557/ The band is selected by the INVOICE's own date across the full schedule: none before May 2024, then NIS 25,000 (May 2024), 20,000 (Jan 2025), 10,000 (Jan 2026), 5,000 (Jun 2026).
5. **Invoice must be issued in the BUYER's name**, but with two qualifications the skill must state. The buyer-registration-number requirement is section 47(ב)(1) of the VAT Law, which empowers the Director to permit its omission for classes of dealers or transactions; it is NOT an amount threshold in the VAT Regulations, which contain no invoice-particulars provision at all (those live in the Director's bookkeeping directives). And תקנה 12ג lets the Director allow a deduction on an invoice NOT in the dealer's name for telephone, water, gas, electricity or like expenses, so a utility bill in a landlord's name must be needs_review rather than a hard denial. (vat-reg-12g-not-in-dealer-name)
6. **VAT rate / arithmetic correctness**, standard VAT is 18% since 1 Jan 2025; total must equal net + VAT within rounding tolerance, else flag. Source: Hebrew Wikipedia VAT (Israel), rate table set by Finance Minister order, https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9A_%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%A3_(%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C)

## Should cover (improves correctness; absence will not by itself produce a wrong claim)

1. **Hosting and entertainment (אירוח) input VAT is an ABSOLUTE bar, not a soft rule.** תקנה 16: no deduction for hosting expenses, with the single exception of hosting a person from abroad. This must sit in the deductibility decision path as a hard-false trigger, not merely in an example. Also carry תקנה 15א (employee benefits: meals, housing, gifts, entertainment), deductible only where the input was sold or supplied to the employee and reported as a taxable transaction. (vat-reg-16-hosting-bar, vat-reg-15a-employee-benefits)
2. **Mixed-use partial deduction is תקנה 18(ב), a GENERAL default and not a vehicle rule.** Where the Director has fixed the non-business proportion that determination governs as an assessment; otherwise two-thirds where use is mainly for the business and a quarter where mainly not. A boolean deductible flag cannot carry those outcomes, so the record must also carry a fraction and the provision relied on. (vat-reg-18b-mixed-use-default)
3. **Separate the PURCHASE of a private car from its RUNNING COSTS.** תקנה 14(א) bars input VAT on the sale or import of a private car to a dealer outright, with תקנה 14(ב) carving out a vehicle dealer and vehicles used exclusively for driving instruction, rental, passenger transport or organised tours. Running costs including fuel are a תקנה 18(ב) mixed-use question, not a vehicle rule. A commercial vehicle outside the private-car definition is not caught by תקנה 14 at all. A one-outcome rendering is a coverage gap. (vat-reg-14a-private-car-no-deduction, vat-reg-14b-carveouts, vat-reg-18b-mixed-use-default)
4. **6-month deduction deadline**, input VAT must be claimed within 6 months of invoice issue; capture invoice date so stale invoices can be flagged. Source: Kol Zchut, מס תשומות, https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/מס_תשומות
5. **Date-aware "current" threshold selection**, the allocation threshold depends on the invoice date, not today's date; the tool must pick the band by the invoice's own date. Source: ACI, https://aci.org.il/knowledge/allocation-number-input-tax-2026/
6. **Foreign-vendor receipts are not Israeli tax invoices**, AWS/Apple/Google etc. carry no Israeli VAT; deduction only via reverse-charge self-report, never as a normal input-VAT line. Source: Kol Zchut, מס תשומות, https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/מס_תשומות
7. **Osek-number check digit**, an individual dealer's osek number is their ID number, whose check digit is Luhn (1-2-1-2 weights); validate it to catch OCR errors, and treat a failure as re-read and review. Company (H.P.) numbers are checked for 9 digits only; no check-digit rule for them is sourced. (osek-hp-9-digits, israeli-id-luhn-check-digit, vatbr-9a-a1-dealer-id-number)

## Out of scope

- Bank-statement reconciliation, matching receipts to bank/credit lines (explicitly excluded in description).
- Free-form handwritten notes with no printed issuer. (Re-scoped 2026-10-29: a pre-printed invoice book with handwritten amounts is a lawful tax invoice and IS in scope, with `handwritten_fields` and needs_review.)
- Filing/transmitting the periodic מע"מ return to the ITA (the tool prepares data, it does not file).
- Income-tax expense deductibility (a separate question from input-VAT deductibility). Re-litigated 2026-10-29: the computation stays out, but the skill now TELLS the user the two questions differ and routes to israeli-expense-categorizer, because a user reading `vat_deductible: false` would otherwise discard a receipt that still matters for income tax.
- Validating allocation numbers against the ITA's live SHAAM service (the tool reads the printed number; it does not call the API).

## Added 2026-08-27 (v1.5.0)

- [x] Six-month deduction window, section 38(א) of the VAT Law. A retroactively obtained allocation number does not restart it (section 47(א2)(4)). Load-bearing for a shoebox-digitising tool. (vat-38a-six-month-window)
- [x] The cash-law consequence is statutory: section 38(א2) disallows input VAT where a section 9 penalty was imposed, with an escape if the dealer proves tax was duly paid. Cash caps are 6,000 NIS or 10% of the transaction, whichever is lower, with a business; 15,000 between non-businesses; 50,000 for a private vehicle sale. (vat-38a2-cash-law-disallowance, cash-law-caps-2022)
- [x] Zero-rated transactions are exempt from the allocation requirement, section 47(א2)(1). (vat-47a2-zero-rate-carveout)
- [x] A missing allocation number disallows the BUYER's deduction and does NOT invalidate the invoice. Invalidity is section 50. The seller need only request a number at the buyer's demand, and an empty field may mean allocation was refused under section 47(א2)(3). (vat-38a1-allocation-disallows-deduction, vat-50-invalid-document-is-separate, vat-47a2-3-refusal)
- [x] Osek patur turnover ceiling, NIS 122,833, section 1 of the VAT Law. (vat-osek-patur-ceiling)
- [ ] Out of scope (explicit), reviewed 2026-08-27: document-type numeric codes. Different systems number document types differently, so a bare code would be misread. The skill names documents instead. (Specific codes removed 2026-10-29: they had no source.)
- [ ] UNRESOLVED, re-reviewed 2026-10-29: the amount above which a tax invoice must carry the buyer's registration number. Section 47(ב)(1) makes the buyer's number the DEFAULT on every tax invoice, with any omission resting on a Director's permit whose general amount is not openly published. The only published small-sum figure is regulation 9א(ג)(1) of the VAT Bookkeeping Regulations (cash-register slip treated as a tax invoice for a SERVICE up to NIS 309 incl. VAT, for dealers under section 4 of schedule 11); it is not a general buyer-number threshold and the skill says so. Original note follows. It is set in the Director's bookkeeping directives, not in the law or the regulations (the phrase סכום קטן appears zero times in both), and no openly readable figure could be sourced. The skill names the threshold without a figure. Do NOT let a future cycle fill it in with a guess.
- [ ] NOT APPLICABLE, reviewed 2026-08-27: תקנה 18(ג), the self-use full-deduction election. The consolidated regulations mark it (בוטלה), repealed with effect from 1.4.1982; its text survives only inside the amendment-history apparatus. Do not reinstate it.
- [ ] CONFIRMED ABSENT, reviewed 2026-08-27: there is no FUTURE (2027) allocation threshold. NIS 5,000 is the standing figure in section 38(א1) and the phase-in is complete.
- [x] CORRECTED 2026-08-27: the earlier note reasoned from "25,000 and 10,000 appear zero times in the consolidated statute" toward the past as well as the future. That inference does not hold backwards. A consolidated statute shows only the CURRENT schedule, so a superseded band leaves no trace in it while still governing every document dated in its window. The historical bands are NIS 25,000 from 5 May 2024 and NIS 20,000 from January 2025, corroborated by icount and greeninvoice, and the Tax Authority FAQ states the full schedule (25,000 for 2024, 20,000 for 2025, 10,000 from January 2026, 5,000 from June 2026). (CORRECTED 2026-10-29: an earlier version of this note quoted a "transitional bracket" as statutory text of section 38(א1); the consolidated text contains no such bracket, and the quote has been removed.) For a shoebox-digitising tool the historical bands are the load-bearing ones.


## Added 2026-10-29 (v1.6.0)

- [x] Must cover: the CLAIMANT's own VAT status, VAT number and business type, asked once per batch (Step 0). An osek patur or a person with no business deducts nothing; without the claimant's number the buyer test cannot pass; business type overrides the "inherently private" defaults. (vat-38a-six-month-window for the "עוסק" entitlement)
- [x] Must cover: batch de-duplication and payment-evidence linking (Step 8). Input VAT is deducted once per tax invoice; a קבלה, a שובר אשראי or a pump slip next to its tax invoice is not a second expense.
- [x] Must cover: retention periods, VAT Law section 75 and Bookkeeping Directives section 25(ג), and the annex F / G conditions for replacing and destroying paper (Step 9, references/retention-and-scanning.md). (vat-75-retention-seven-years, bkd-25c-retention, bkd-25h2-scan-accepted-if-annex-f-g, bkd-annex-f-*)
- [x] CORRECTED: regulation 18(א) (actual business ratio) is the primary mixed-use rule; 18(ב)'s two-thirds and one-quarter apply only where non-business use is indeterminate. `deductible_fraction` now takes any value from 0 to 1. (vat-reg-18a-actual-ratio, vat-reg-18b-indeterminate-use)
- [x] CORRECTED: the six-month window is not absolute; section 116(א) lets the Director extend it up to five years. (vat-116a-extension-five-years)
- [x] Allocation number format: 9 digits, random, no check digit (Tax Authority FAQ); a missing number may be requested up to one year after the invoice date. A brief claimed a "26-character, A30-prefix" format; no Tax Authority page read this cycle says so, and it was not encoded. If a longer string is printed, the skill keeps it verbatim and routes to review rather than guessing which digits count. The regime applies to invoices dated from 5 May 2024. (allocation-number-9-digits-no-check-digit, allocation-request-up-to-one-year)
- [x] VAT rate is date-aware: 17% before 1.1.2025, 18% from then. (vat-rate-18)
- [x] Cash Law: the "10% or cap, whichever is lower" rule applies to the private (15,000) and vehicle (50,000) caps too. (cash-law-private-10pct-or-15000-vehicle-50000)
- [x] Regulation 15א's "עובד" includes an owner or manager, so a sole trader's own meal is caught. (vat-reg-15a-owner-is-employee)
- [ ] Out of scope (explicit), reviewed 2026-10-29: VAT return line mapping (which return field an input goes in). The skill groups deductible inputs by invoice month; mapping to return fields is the bookkeeper's job and no primary source for the field layout was read this cycle.
- [ ] Out of scope (explicit), reviewed 2026-10-29: section 47א (a dealer's duty to DEMAND a tax invoice above NIS 362). It is advice for future purchases, not a deduction rule, and does not change any record's outcome.
- [ ] CONFIRMED ABSENT, re-reviewed 2026-10-29: no allocation band after June 2026 (NIS 5,000 in section 38(א1), consolidated to 13-07-2026). Secondary sources still quoting 15,000 for 2026 and 10,000 for 2027 reflect a superseded 2024 schedule.
- [x] Added in review round 1 (2026-10-29): an amuta (מלכ"ר) and a financial institution cannot deduct (section 1 "עוסק" excludes them); credit notes are netted (regulation 24(ב), section 43); regulation 15 home-purchase bar; section 41 exempt-supply inputs; the Cash Law limit applies only above NIS 6,000; a three-state `decision` field (deductible / review / denied) drives the buckets.
