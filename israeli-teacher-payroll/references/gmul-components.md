# Gmul components for teachers

A "gmul" (increment) is a percentage add-on to the combined salary (sachar
meshulav = base rank pay plus seniority). Gmulim are where teacher pay diverges
most from private-sector payroll, and they are the components an LLM most often
forgets. Percentages below are verified; the NIS amount they produce depends on
the combined salary from the reform's table, so compute the base first.

## First split: how the reform treats increments

- **Ofek Chadash**: teachers do NOT accumulate hishtalmut or merit gmulim. They
  accumulate professional-development hours that push them up the 9-rank ladder;
  rank IS the reward mechanism. BUT role gmulim (gmulei tafkid) DO still apply,
  for example gmul chinuch for a homeroom teacher or gmul nihul gan for a
  kindergarten manager. The "no gmulim" rule is about banked study/merit gmulim,
  not about role gmulim.
- **Oz LaTmura**: teachers can accumulate at most ONE gmul per year (sabbatical,
  half-sabbatical and unpaid leave excepted), and the hishtalmut quota is 19
  units. Rank advancement runs on merit points (nekudot zechut) rather than on the
  Ofek dev-hour quota; read the current points-to-rank conversion from the
  ministry's "nekudot zechut ve-dargot kidum" page rather than assuming a fixed
  percentage, since the older 10-points-equals-2% formulation is not stated on any
  live official page we could reach.

## Gmul types and their basis

| Gmul | Rate | Basis / notes |
|------|------|---------------|
| Gmul hishtalmut (professional development) | 1.2% per unit, 1.3% from 1.9.2025 | 112 study hours (or equivalent) with a passing grade = 1 unit. The Oz quota rose to 19 units in tashaf (1.9.2019), from 18; at most one unit banked per year (sabbatical / half-sabbatical / unpaid leave excepted). |
| Gmul chinuch (homeroom / class education) | 10% of combined salary OR 1,000 NIS, whichever is higher | The 1,000 NIS floor applies from 1.9.2024 and binds for a low-rank teacher, so a bare 10% understates. Grade-1 (alef) homeroom teachers keep a higher 11.5%. |
| Gmul rikuz miktzoa, OZ (subject coordinator) | 8%, or 9% for mathematics and English | From 1.9.2025. Ofek is 6% elementary / 8% junior-high, see below. |
| Gmul yeutz (educational counsellor) | 12% temporary licence, 18% permanent | From 1.9.2024 the rate is keyed to the licence type, NOT to seniority in counselling. |
| Gmul chinuch meyuchad, OZ ONLY (special education) | 10%, 15%, or 17% | From 1.9.2024, raised from 9% / 14% / 14%. The 17% band is for autism or severe-psychiatric settings. The Ofek bands are DIFFERENT (8.5 / 15 / 17), see the Ofek table below. |
| Gmul yozma chinuchit beit-sifrit (Ofek Chadash) | 400 to 1,000 NIS a month | In force from 1.9.2026 (tashpaz). 2 to 5 units per approved initiative at the principal's discretion, each unit 200 NIS a month. Teachers and appointed deputy principals. Fixed-shekel, NOT a percentage, so it must not be passed to the script as a gmul percentage. Re-decided every school year. This is the SAME gmul the union's standing terms page lists as 'gmul tafkid beit-sifri (from September 2025), 200 NIS per unit, up to 5 units per teacher, up to 36,000 NIS per school': September 2025 was the agreed date, 1.9.2026 is the announced entry into force, and the 2-unit minimum announced on 16.8.2026 governs, so a single 200 NIS unit is not payable. |
| Gmul pituach miktzoi (professional-development role) | 6% of combined salary | Role-based. |
| Gmul nihul gan (kindergarten management) | 17% (up to 5 years of management seniority, ranks 1-2), 20% (6 to 10 years, ranks 3-4), 21% (over 10 years, ranks 5-9) | An Ofek Chadash role. EVERY band carries a floor of 1,500 NIS, paid if higher than the percentage. A gananet amita draws 10%, unless she carries a management gmul or salary protection as a gan manager. |

read the one matching the teacher's reform. Ofek also splits several rates

Every role gmul is computed on a full position EXCEPT the special-education and
inclusion gmulim, which are computed on the share of frontal hours taught in the
special-education setting.

Appointment conditions: a teacher must be certified, employed at least a third of
a position in frontal teaching, and on the school's staff; some roles demand more.

**The two-gmul cap, precisely.** A teacher who is neither a first deputy nor a
principal may hold at most two role gmulim. A school principal may not draw a role
gmul at all. A first deputy may serve only as a homeroom teacher. For a second
deputy or above, the deputy gmul itself counts as one of the two.

**Retention grant.** A one-off 10,000 NIS manak shimur is paid in the September
2026 salary to a teacher whose employment began 1.9.2023. It is a grant, not a
salary component.

Separately, Oz roles carry promotion points (nekudot zechut) toward rank

rakaz yeutz and rakaz maarechet were added at 2.5 each.

## Other gmulim not in the table above

- **Gmul nihul / sgan menahel (school principal and deputy)**: principals and
  their deputies earn a management gmul that is distinct from the kindergarten
  gan-management gmul. Read the current rate from the union; do not invent one.
- **English, mathematics, and physics**: these subjects sit under separate
  incentive and coordination arrangements of their own, which is exactly why the
  subject-coordinator gmul above is scoped to subjects OTHER than English and
  mathematics.

## Ceiling rule

A teacher is entitled to at most TWO role gmulim (gmulei tafkid). Do not stack
every eligible role gmul; apply the reform's cap.

## Where the actual NIS number comes from

The gmul percentage multiplies the combined salary, which comes from the
official rank/seniority table for the specific reform and year. Because those
per-rank NIS cells change with each wage agreement, read them from the union
salary table or the Ministry of Education salary-simulation calculator rather
than from a frozen table here:

- Official calculator: https://poh.education.gov.il/administrative/salary/salary-sheet/salary-simulation-calculator/
- Gmulei hishtalmut: https://poh.education.gov.il/MerhavMinhali/HskalaVetek/Pages/GmuleiHishtalmut.aspx
- Histadrut HaMorim gmulei tafkid: https://www.itu.org.il/
