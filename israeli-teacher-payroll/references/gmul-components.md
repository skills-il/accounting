# Gmul components for teachers

A "gmul" (increment) is a percentage add-on to the combined salary (sachar
meshulav = base rank pay plus seniority). Gmulim are where teacher pay diverges
most from private-sector payroll, and they are the components an LLM most often
forgets. Percentages below are verified; the NIS amount they produce depends on
the combined salary from the reform's table, so compute the base first.

## First split: how the reform treats increments

- **Ofek Chadash**: teachers do NOT accumulate hishtalmut or merit gmulim. They
  accumulate professional-development hours that push them up the 9-rank ladder;
  rank IS the reward mechanism. BUT role gmulim (gmulei tafkid) DO still apply,
  for example gmul chinuch for a homeroom teacher or gmul nihul gan for a
  kindergarten manager. The "no gmulim" rule is about banked study/merit gmulim,
  not about role gmulim.
- **Oz LaTmura**: teachers can accumulate at most ONE gmul per year (sabbatical,
  half-sabbatical and unpaid leave excepted), and the hishtalmut quota is 19
  units. Advancement runs on promotion grades earned with merit points (nekudot zechut) rather than on the
  Ofek dev-hour quota. Per the ministry's "nekudot zechut ve-dargot kidum" page,
  every 10 points earns a promotion grade of 1.67% of the combined salary, at most
  4 grades over a career (6.69% cumulative), at least 3 years between grades, and
  a teacher holding several roles accrues points for one role only. The older
  "10 points equals 2%, up to 8%" formulation is wrong.

## Ofek Chadash rank promotion

Rank 1 is a new teacher. Promotion turns on three criteria together: paznun (the
required months in rank), shapam (professional-development hours), and an
evaluation. Ranks 2 and 3 need 30 months and 120 hours; ranks 4, 5 and 6 need 36
months and 180 hours; ranks 7, 8 and 9 need 48 months and 210 hours. Promotion
quotas for ranks 7 to 9 were abolished in the 2016 agreement. This is
Ofek-specific: the 9 ranks are an Ofek Chadash structure, and Oz LaTmura advances by promotion grades instead.

Every role gmul is computed on a full position EXCEPT the special-education and
inclusion gmulim, which are computed on the share of frontal hours taught in the
special-education setting.

Appointment conditions: a teacher must be certified, employed at least a third of
a position in frontal teaching, and on the school's staff; some roles demand more.

**The two-gmul cap, precisely.** A teacher who is neither a first deputy nor a
principal may hold at most two role gmulim. A school principal may not draw a role
gmul at all. A first deputy may serve only as a homeroom teacher. For a second
deputy or above, the deputy gmul itself counts as one of the two.

**Retention grant.** A one-off 10,000 NIS manak shimur for every new teacher who
started work on or after 1.9.2023. The 19.10.2022 agreement conditions it on staying until
kviut with a position of 70% or more at that date; below a full position it is paid
in proportion to the weighted position over the 36 months before payment. The union
page says the September 2026 salary, the agreement says the May after kviut, so
confirm the month. It is a grant, not a salary component; its pension and tax
treatment are not published.

The 6% rakaz gmulim (miyumanuyot and the section 17(א) roles) are computed on the
combined salary PLUS the Oz reform supplement (tosefet Oz LaTmura), per the
ministry's Oz page and the payroll provider's summary. The bundled script has a
single base, so compute these by hand and pass the shekel result with `--fixed`.

Separately, Oz roles carry promotion points (nekudot zechut) toward rank
advancement, listed on the ministry's Oz reform page: rakaz shichva 4; rakaz
chinuch chevrati and rakaz miktzoa 3; rakaz haaracha u-medida, rakaz hataamot
le-bchinot ha-bagrut and rakaz tiyulim 2.5; mechanech kita 2. From 1.9.2025 rakaz
pedagogi, rakaz hishtalvut, rakaz tikshuv, rakaz bagrut, rakaz mishmaat ve-aklim,
rakaz yeutz and rakaz maarechet were added at 2.5 each.

## Other gmulim not in the table above

- **Gmul nihul / sgan menahel (school principal and deputy)**: principals and
  their deputies earn a management gmul that is distinct from the kindergarten
  gan-management gmul. Read the current rate from the union; do not invent one.
- **English, mathematics, and physics**: these subjects sit under separate
  incentive arrangements of their own (for example the Oz physics bagrut gmul
  above). They do NOT remove the coordinator gmul: an Oz maths or English rakaz
  miktzoa draws 9%.
- **Gmul yeutz at part time**: the pre-reform terms of service prorate it by
  counselling hours (full at 12 weekly hours, none under 4). Whether the same
  holds under Ofek or Oz was not confirmed on a live page, so check the slip.

## Oz LaTmura: the shekel supplement and the 31.8.2026 agreement

- **Tosefet shiklit Oz 2023** (agreement of 23.9.2024, section 7), full position:
  1,200 NIS from 1.9.2024, 1,518 NIS from 1.9.2025, 1,790.50 NIS from 1.9.2026, and
  2,066.75 NIS scheduled from 1.9.2027 in the signed table (no execution circular
  for 2027 yet). Section 7(ג): prorated by position fraction, including above
  100%. Section 7(ד): it is salary for all purposes, including pension and keren
  hishtalmut deposits; 7(ה) it does not count toward the minimum-wage top-up; 7(ו)
  it is not a base for any other supplement; 7(ז) it counts for menak yovel. Script: `--fixed-prorated`. A separate older line, "tosefet shiklit
  2016", may also appear on an Oz slip.
- **Irgun HaMorim agreement of 31.8.2026**, in force to 31.8.2029: the physics
  bagrut gmul above; in school year tashpaz only, up to four substitution hours
  for a full position with no extra pay (pro rata for part time); a one-off annual gmul shiluv for an Oz
  homeroom teacher whose tashpav class had pupils on a personal basket, 600 NIS
  for one such pupil or 2,000 NIS for more than one, with no pension deposits,
  paid in the September 2026 salary and no later than October 2026 (conditions:
  six months of homeroom work, gmul chinuch received, at least a third of a
  position); and no pay deduction for the 8.6.2026 strike day.

## Ceiling rule

A teacher is entitled to at most TWO role gmulim (gmulei tafkid). Do not stack
every eligible role gmul; apply the reform's cap.

## Where the actual NIS number comes from

The gmul percentage multiplies the combined salary, which comes from the
official rank/seniority table for the specific reform and year. Because those
per-rank NIS cells change with each wage agreement, read them from the union
salary table or the Ministry of Education salary-simulation calculator rather
than from a frozen table here:

- Official calculator: https://poh.education.gov.il/administrative/salary/salary-sheet/salary-simulation-calculator/
- Gmulei hishtalmut: https://poh.education.gov.il/MerhavMinhali/HskalaVetek/Pages/GmuleiHishtalmut.aspx
- Histadrut HaMorim gmulei tafkid: https://www.itu.org.il/
