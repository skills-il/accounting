# Reform hour structure: Ofek Chadash vs Oz LaTmura

Every teacher salary starts from the reform's work-week definition. Get the
reform and the school level right first, because the number of frontal hours,
private hours, and the total position all change between them.

## Two hour concepts you must not mix

- **Frontal hour (shaat horaa frontalit / shaot pruntaliyot)**: standing in
  front of a class (also role hours like homeroom or Bagrut prep in Oz).
- **Private hour (shaa pratanit)**: teaching a single student or a small group.
  Ofek: up to 5 students. Oz: up to 3 students, or up to 5 by a pedagogical-team
  decision.
- **Stay / support hour (shaat shehiya in Ofek, shaa tomechet horaa in Oz)**:
  time at school for meetings, prep, parent talks, and checking work. Paid, but
  it is NOT front-of-class teaching.

The full weekly position is the SUM of all three. A common error is to treat
only the frontal hours as "the job".

## Ofek Chadash (kindergarten + elementary + junior-high)

Full 100% position = 36 weekly hours.

| Level | Frontal | Private | Stay | Total |
|-------|---------|---------|------|-------|
| Elementary (yesodi) | 26 | 5 | 5 | 36 |
| Junior-high (chativat beynayim) | 23 | 4 | 9 | 36 |

Kindergarten (gan) teachers are also under Ofek Chadash: the reform runs in
kindergartens, elementary schools, and middle schools. The gan work-week is
defined separately from the two school rows above, so read the current gan
structure from the union or the ministry rather than reusing an elementary row.

Source: education.gov.il Portal Ovdei Horaa work-week page; union structure PDF;
mosdot.education.gov.il Ofek reform page and pob.education.gov.il kindergarten
Ofek page for the kindergarten coverage.

## Oz LaTmura (upper-secondary only)

Base position historically = 40 weekly hours.

| Component | Hours | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Frontal | 24 | Includes role hours (homeroom, Bagrut prep) |
| Private | 6 | Group up to 3 (up to 5 by pedagogical decision) |
| Teaching-support | 10 | See breakdown below |
| **Total** | **40** | |

Ten support hours: work meetings 2/wk, team training 2/wk, parent talks 1/wk,
prep and checking work and exams 5/wk.

**Change from tashpe (school year 2024/25):** the full position becomes 38
weekly hours, with 25 frontal and 3 private hours (the 10 support hours stay).
Older payslips and older tables still show the 40-hour base, so check the year
before you read a table. The 38-hour split is stated directly on the Ministry of
Education Oz reform page, so it no longer needs a payroll-provider cross-check.

**Age bands (Oz only).** The required hours fall with age:

| Age | Frontal | Total |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Under 50 | 25 | 38 |
| 50 to 55 | 23 | 36 |
| Over 55 | 21 | 34 |

**Ofek Chadash has age reductions too**, expressed as hours taken off rather than
a replacement total: a serving teacher from age 50 loses 2 frontal hours; from 55
loses 4 hours (3 frontal plus 1 private); a newly hired teacher from 50 loses 2. A
kindergarten teacher in a chativa tzeira is treated as a school teacher for this.
Both reforms' reductions are for high position fractions and scale down
proportionally below that. Missing either scores an older teacher as
under-working a full position.

**Two more Ofek work-week variants** the elementary/junior-high rows above do
not cover: a mother-teacher at 79% or 80% and up loses one frontal and one stay
hour; and a shiluv or para-medical teaching worker runs 26 frontal + 3 private + 7
stay in elementary, or 23 + 2 + 11 in a junior-high, with travel between schools
counted as stay time.

## Salary unification (ichud maskorot) for a split appointment

Per the Ministry's salary-consolidation page: kviut at the employer that unifies
(the Ministry or the baalut), plus at least a third position in the junior-high to
unify through the Ministry, or at least a half position in the high school to unify
through the baalut, in a six-year school sharing one institution code. Kviut at
both employers in the same year means unification where the position is larger;
kviut at the baalut first blocks unification at the Ministry. A high-school
principal, a junior-high principal and a first deputy in a junior-high are not
covered. When the two parts together exceed 100% of a position, unification can
lose benefits capped at 100%, such as havraa, bigud and meonot.

## Havraa days by teaching seniority

Teachers are paid the PUBLIC-sector havraa day rate, not the private one. Days
for a full position, proportional for part-time:

| Seniority | Havraa days |
|-----------|-------------|
| First 3 years | 7 |
| 4 to 10 years | 9 |
| 11 to 15 years | 10 |
| 16 to 19 years | 11 |
| 20 to 24 years | 12 |
| 25 years and over | 13 |

The day rate: per the Commissioner on Wages circular of 8.6.2026 the public-sector
day is 511.60 NIS from 1.6.2026, up from 471.40 NIS. The 451.50 NIS agreed for the
private sector on 22.6.2026 does not bind teachers; using it understates every day.

## Pension layers on a teacher slip

The Ministry pension page (stated as of 1.6.2018) lists four rows: pensia vatika
(employer 7.5% + 6% severance, employee 7%); pensia tzoveret (employer 7.5% +
6.224% severance, employee 6% or 7%, her choice); expense reimbursements such as
travel and phone (employer 5%, employee 5% to 7%); and work above 100% (employer
7.5% + 8.33% severance, employee 5% to 7%). So a slip can carry two or three
pension lines, including one on the travel allowance. A teacher who gave birth
during the school year has retroactive pension, provident and keren deductions
taken from her July salary.

## July and August

July and August are paid as holiday pay in proportion to the months actually
worked in the year, and no pay component changes in those months (a gmul cannot
take effect in July or August).

## Bigud and the context baselines

The annual clothing allowance (bigud) is 2,527 NIS for a full position at
seniority level 4 in 2026. The 91 NIS cut and the de-indexation that produced
2,314 NIS in 2025 (from 2,405 NIS in 2024) were a one-time measure for that year
only, so do not carry them forward.

Minimum wage is 6,443.85 NIS a month from 1.4.2026 (6,247.67 NIS from 1.4.2025);
the hourly rate is 35.40 NIS. The average wage is 13,769 NIS
(Bituach Leumi section 2, January 2026). All of these are revised each year
(minimum wage each April, the average wage and the BL steps each January); the
2027 figures are not published yet. Teacher base pay comes from the agreement
table, not from these figures.

**Position ceiling.** Maximum employment across all institutions together is
100%. A mother-teacher (morah em) may go to 100% actual work plus a 7% mother
supplement. Above 100% is possible only with ministry approval, in frontal hours
only, up to 12 extra frontal hours (131.5% in total), and never above 50 weekly
hours in any case.

## Practical implication

Two teachers with the same rank and seniority, one in Ofek elementary and one in
Oz upper-secondary, sit on DIFFERENT base-pay tables and have different position
sizes. Never carry a number from one reform to the other. When someone says "I
teach 24 hours", ask which reform and which hours (frontal only, or the full
position), because 25 frontal in Oz has been a full position since tashpe (24 before that),
while 25 in Ofek is not.
