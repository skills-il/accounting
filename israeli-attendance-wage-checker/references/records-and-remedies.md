# Records, the burden of proof, the payslip, and late payment

This file is the procedural half of the skill, and for an employee with no paperwork it is worth more
than the arithmetic.

## 1. The employer must keep an hours ledger

`סעיף 25` of חוק שעות עבודה ומנוחה: an employer must keep a ledger of work hours, weekly rest hours,
overtime, overtime premium, and weekly-rest premium.

The record must be kept on an ongoing basis and must contain hours **actually worked**. Where it is
not produced by mechanical, digital, or electronic means, it must be signed daily by the employee and
approved by a designated supervisor. A spreadsheet reconstructed after the fact is not a ledger.

## 2. The burden shift, and its two limits

`סעיף 26ב(א)` of חוק הגנת השכר, which is the strongest lever an employee has:

> בתובענה של עובד לתשלום שכר עבודה, לרבות גמול שעות נוספות או גמול עבודה במנוחה השבועית, שבה שנויות
> במחלוקת שעות העבודה שבעדן נתבע השכר, תהא חובת ההוכחה על המעסיק כי העובד לא עמד לרשות העבודה במשך
> שעות העבודה השנויות במחלוקת, אם המעסיק לא הציג רישומי נוכחות מתוך פנקס שעות עבודה, ככל שהוא חייב
> לנהלו

So where the hours are disputed and the employer produced no attendance records, the employer must
prove the employee was **not** at his disposal.

**Two limits the skill must always state alongside it:**

1. **The cap.** `סעיף 26ב(ב)` confines the shifted burden to **no more than 15 weekly overtime hours
   or 60 monthly overtime hours**. "No records means the employee wins everything" is wrong, and a
   skill that implies it sets the user up for disappointment.
2. **The employee must still say something.** The shift does not excuse the employee from putting
   forward a minimal factual version of the hours he says he worked. Where he fails to, no premium is
   awarded even in the absence of attendance reports.

Report the result as a **bounded range** anchored on the cap, not as a single confident figure.

## 3. Inclusive wage is treated as regular wage only

`סעיף 5` of חוק הגנת השכר: where a wage was set that includes payment for overtime or weekly-rest
premium (or for annual leave), the wage set is treated as **regular wage only**, absent a collective
agreement approved by the Minister.

Practical effect: "your salary already covers the overtime" does not extinguish the entitlement. The
salary is re-characterised as base pay and the premium is computed on top.

## 4. The payslip, and what its gaps trigger

The Schedule to חוק הגנת השכר (via `סעיף 24`) requires the payslip to state, among other things:

- the number of work days and work hours at the workplace in the period;
- the days actually worked;
- **the hours actually worked**, with an express note where the employee is one to whom the
  supervision exclusion applies;
- **the value of a regular work hour**;
- other payments itemised, **including overtime and weekly-rest premium, with the number of units and
  the amount**.

These are exactly the fields the reconciliation reads. Their absence is both a breach in itself and,
under `סעיף 26ב(ג)`, a trigger: where the disputed causes are overtime, weekly-rest premium, leave,
recuperation or travel, and the employer gave no payslip or one omitting those components, it is
**presumed** that an unlawful inclusive wage was set and the wage paid is treated as regular wage
exclusive of them.

That presumption converts a bad payslip directly into a computable gap, which is why the skill should
always ask to see the payslip before concluding there is nothing to work with.

## 5. Late payment

A monthly wage is payable **at the end of the month for which it is paid** (`סעיף 9`). Wage that goes
unpaid past the statutory point becomes `שכר מולן`.

`סעיף 17` sets the compensation as **the higher of** two formulas:

1. one twentieth of the delayed wage for the first week after the due date, and one tenth for each
   further week or part of a week; or
2. index differences from the due date to payment, **plus 20 percent** on the combined total of the
   delayed wage and those index differences, for each month in the period.

Compute both and take the larger. Quoting only the first understates it on a long delay; quoting only
the second understates it on a short one.

### The clock, which is short and separate

`סעיף 17א`: the right to **delayed-wage compensation**, as distinct from the wage itself, lapses if no
claim is filed within **one year** from when the wage is regarded as delayed, **or within 60 days**
from the day the employee received the related wage, **whichever is earlier**. The court may extend
the 60 days to 90.

Two consequences to state every time a halana figure is produced:

- The underlying **wage claim** survives far longer than the halana right. A user who has missed the
  halana window has usually not lost the wage.
- A labour court may **reduce or cancel** halana compensation on a genuine mistake, a circumstance
  outside the employer's control, or a bona fide dispute over the debt, provided the undisputed part
  was paid on time. So the figure is a **claim ceiling**, not an award. Present it that way.

## 6. Ordering of output

Produce the reconciliation in this order, because it is the order in which the user can act:

1. The applicability gate result (does the law even apply to this worker).
2. Gross owed against gross paid, itemised by cause.
3. Compliance breaches, in a separate section, clearly not framed as entitlements.
4. Records position: what exists, what the employer was obliged to keep, and what follows.
5. Timing: which clocks are running and which have expired.
6. Hand-off: what a labour court would decide and what needs an advocate.
